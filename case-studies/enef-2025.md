# ENEF 2025, financing a national student congress

National Congress of Physics Students, Basque Country, September 2024 to August 2025. Co-director of the organisation and finance lead.

## Contents

- [The project](#the-project)
- [What was managed](#what-was-managed)
- [Outcome](#outcome)

## The project

A five-day congress for about 200 physics students, run by an organising team of more than twenty people. Two co-directors led the team. The finance committee had six people and a single lead.

## What was managed

- The project proposal and the sponsorship dossiers.
- Contact with about thirty companies and public bodies until agreements closed.
- The spending plan for accommodation, venues and activities, and the dealings with suppliers.
- Media interviews on behalf of the congress.

## Outcome

More than 80,000 euros raised, with a substantial contribution from a public body (Lantik).

## Related student-association work

- ZIMATEK, the physics and electronic engineering students' association: board secretary 2022 to 2025, more than twelve contests and talks a year, LaTeX courses for more than a hundred people.
- Co-founder and secretary of the UPV/EHU delegation of the RSEF student group, running the local phases of national physics competitions.
- Local phase of the Physics Olympiad in Euskadi: organiser and trainer.
- School science outreach in Bizkaia, now co-coordinated with a team of four.
