## BCAM, physics-informed neural networks

August to October 2024, Basque Center for Applied Mathematics, MATHDES line. Built physics-informed neural networks in Python to simulate the motion of water for renewable-energy applications. Presented progress at group meetings and wrote literature reviews.

## Bisky Team, launch simulation

September 2023 to September 2024, student rocketry team of UPV/EHU, control and simulation team. Wrote Python launch simulations used to reduce risk before two real suborbital launches, and prepared progress reports for the team and for international rocketry competition applications.

## BioRes group, ballistocardiography

September 2024 to June 2026, UPV/EHU. Reviewed research on ballistocardiogram signals and built machine-learning models in MATLAB to estimate cardiac output. See also the [cardiac-arrest classification study](cardiac-arrest-classification.md).

## IXA group, language technology

Since November 2025, UPV/EHU. Natural-language-processing and deep-learning models that read social-media text for early signs of psychological risk, using public research corpora, with a view to an international shared task.
